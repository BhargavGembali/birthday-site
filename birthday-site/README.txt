HOW TO USE
1. Put your photos in the "images" folder, named:
   timeline1.jpg ... timeline5.jpg   (the 5 timeline stops)
   photo1.jpg ... photo6.jpg         (the gallery)
2. Put your songs in the "songs" folder: song1.mp3, song2.mp3, song3.mp3
3. Open config.js in any text editor (Notepad is fine) and replace the
   [PLACEHOLDERS] with your real names, captions, memories and song details.
   Different filenames or more photos/songs? Just edit the lists in config.js.
4. Double-click index.html to preview it in your browser.

TO SHARE IT
Upload the whole folder to a free host (Netlify Drop: app.netlify.com/drop,
or GitHub Pages / Vercel) and send her the link. Keep the folder structure as is.

TIPS
- Resize photos to ~1600px wide so the page loads fast.
- Test the full experience once yourself before sending, with sound on.
